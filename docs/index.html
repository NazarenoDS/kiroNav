FAQ

What is KiroNav?

KiroNav is a new tool for the Kiro ecosystem that extends AI assistance beyond code. While Kiro helps developers write software, KiroNav helps everyone else USE software — with real-time, step-by-step visual guidance powered by the same AI models.

Who is it for?

Anyone who struggles with technology: elderly users, non-technical employees, people onboarding into new tools, or anyone who'd rather have a patient guide than watch a 20-minute tutorial video. KiroNav opens a massive market for Kiro beyond developers.

How is this different from tutorials or Clippy?

Tutorials are generic — they don't know what's on YOUR screen. KiroNav sees your actual screen in real time, identifies your exact context, and gives you personalized instructions. It adapts if you're stuck and advances automatically when you succeed.

Does it take control of my computer?

Never. KiroNav only observes and guides. It tells you what to click — you do it yourself. The philosophy is teaching, not automating. The user learns and becomes independent.

What's the vision for Kiro?

Kiro already owns the developer experience. KiroNav extends that to the entire tech market — every person who uses a computer but doesn't know how to do something. It's a natural product expansion: same brand, same AI, new audience of millions.

What AI model does it use?

Claude Sonnet 4.5 with vision capability through the Kiro models catalog. Same tokens, same account, same infrastructure. No additional API keys or services needed.

Does it work on mobile?

Desktop is available now (Windows, macOS, Linux) as a floating transparent widget. Mobile (Android) with floating overlay and screen sharing permission is the next milestone — same experience, on your phone.

How does it fit into Kiro's product line?

Kiro IDE helps you code. Kiro CLI helps you in the terminal. Kiro Web helps you remotely. KiroNav helps you USE any software. It's the consumer-facing arm of Kiro — the ghost that follows you everywhere.
